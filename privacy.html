Privacy policy

Your projects stay yours.

Effective October 16, 2026. ScallopPlan is provided by KUN WANG. Contact: Stelyra-Astro@proton.me.

Data in the app

ScallopPlan stores project names, dimensions, notes and marking progress locally on your device. It does not send this information to us. The app has no user accounts, advertising, tracking, developer-operated backend, analytics SDK or AI service. It does not request your location, contacts, microphone, camera or photo library.

Files you choose to share

You can export a PDF or JSON backup, print, or share through the iOS share sheet. The destination you select receives the file. Its handling of that file is governed by that service and your settings. Importing a backup reads the file you choose and, after confirmation, replaces the local library. A recovery copy of the previous library is kept locally.

Device or iCloud backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Delete projects in the app to remove them from its current library. Deleting the app removes its local container; copies you exported, shared, or backed up must be managed separately.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes the paid download. We do not receive payment card details. Apple may provide sales and diagnostic reports through its developer services, depending on your settings. ScallopPlan does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. See Apple's privacy information.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and troubleshoot, not for advertising. We keep them only as long as needed for that purpose, generally no longer than 12 months after the request is resolved, unless an ongoing dispute or legal obligation requires longer retention. You may request earlier deletion by emailing us. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless needed for your request.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising scripts, forms or cookies. GitHub may process technical connection information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect the hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Updates and questions

If these practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Contact Stelyra-Astro@proton.me with questions about this policy or support correspondence.
