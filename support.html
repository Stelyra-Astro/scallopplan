Start with a straight, flat edge

Measure the full length. Set a straight margin at either end, then choose how many scallop repeats to fit into the remaining run. Adjust the depth to change the shape. Inch fields accept fractions such as 2 1/2. Changing display units preserves your measurements.

Round uses circular arcs; Soft uses a smooth wave. Valley ends begin and finish between scallops. Peak ends split a repeat into two halves at opposite ends. Straight margins meet those endpoint heights. The app does not draft corners, curved hems, necklines or complete garments.

The template is a trace line, without seam allowance.

Add the allowance needed by your facing, binding or other finishing method. Test on scrap material before cutting your final piece.

Which template should I print?

One repeat makes a reusable stencil without the project's straight margins. Place it using the marking guide. Whole edge includes the straight margins and tiles a full strip across numbered pages, with a 10 mm overlap. Each export also has a settings and printing guide.

How do I keep the printed size correct?

Choose A4 or US Letter in both the app and your printer settings. Print at 100% or Actual Size; turn off Fit to Page and borderless enlargement. Measure the 1-inch square and 50 mm line on every template page with a physical ruler. If either is wrong, correct the print settings before tracing. Your phone screen and PDF preview are not physical rulers.

To assemble tiles, trim one shaded overlap band, align the same global positions and registration lines, and tape on the reverse. Match the printed geometry, not the outside paper edges.

What are the marking positions?

They are measured from the left end of the full edge, including the left margin. Use the absolute start and end positions shown for each repeat, rather than repeatedly adding a rounded repeat width. Check off repeats as you mark them. Changing geometry asks to clear old progress.

Where are my projects stored?

On your device. In Studio, use Export all projects to save a JSON backup to Files. Keep a backup before changing phones or deleting the app. Restore shows a preview and asks before replacing the library. The app keeps a local recovery copy of the previous library, available from Studio after a restore. Device backups follow your Apple settings.

Does it need an account or subscription?

No. ScallopPlan is designed as a one-time paid download with all features included. Planning, saving and generating PDFs work offline. AirPrint, email and cloud destinations may need a connection. Apple handles purchases and refunds.

What devices and languages are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is in English, supports inches and centimeters, and uses a fixed light appearance so the canvas stays consistent.
